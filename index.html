PRIVACY POLICY

DATA STORAGE

All data is stored locally on your device using localStorage.No data is transmitted to any external server except for the optional Google Sheets progress sync.

MENSTRUATION DATA

Menstruation tracking data is stored ONLY on your device and is never sent to any server, including Google Sheets.

GOOGLE SHEETS SYNC

Only Streak count, XP total, and completion status are synced. No personal or sensitive data is included.

NO ANALYTICS

This app contains zero tracking, analytics, or advertising. Your worship data is private and yours alone.
