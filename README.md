# Namazy — The 40-Day Prayer Challenge

**Short Description (80 characters max):**
Track your 5 daily prayers, build streaks & join the 40-day Salah challenge.

---

## Full Description

**Prayer Must be your FIRST priority.**

Namazy is a free, ad-free gamified Islamic prayer tracker built for Muslims who want to be consistent in their daily Salah. Whether you are a student, a working professional, or beginning your Islamic journey — Namazy makes tracking your prayers simple, rewarding, and community-connected.

---

### 🕌 CORE PRAYER TRACKING

- Track all 6 daily prayers: **Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha, and Witr**
- Each prayer includes Fard (mandatory), Sunnah, Jamah, and Mosque sub-tasks
- **Fard tasks are required** to maintain your streak — optional tasks earn bonus XP
- Prayer cards unlock automatically at their correct times based on your location
- Navigate up to **30 days back** to backfill missed prayers before syncing

---

### ⏰ LIVE ATHAN & PRAYER TIMES

- Fetches accurate prayer times using your GPS location via the Aladhan API
- Displays the **time remaining** until each prayer directly on its card
- Highlights the **currently active prayer** period in real time
- Supports **±60 minute custom offset** for each prayer individually (for local accuracy)
- Prayer time reminders via browser notifications

---

### 🔥 STREAK & XP GAMIFICATION

- Build a daily streak — **miss a mandatory Fard = streak resets**
- Earn XP points for every completed task
- Level up through **10 ranks**: Initiate → Deen Master
- View your today's XP, total XP, streak count, and perfect day count on the home screen
- **Backfill protection**: go back up to 30 days to recover missed prayers before they lock

---

### 🛡️ THE 40-DAY SHIELD CHALLENGE

- Male users: **40-day continuous Fard streak** target
- Female users: **30-day challenge** option (accounts for natural cycle)
- Complete the challenge to unlock the **Shield Protocol Authentication**
- Submit your name and testimony upon completion

---

### 🏫 ACADEMY LEADERBOARD

- Designed for **schools, madrasahs, colleges, and universities in Bangladesh**
- Students register with their institution, class, and roll number during onboarding
- Completed students appear in the **Challenge Completers spotlight**
- Institutions are ranked by number of successful students in the **Leaderboard**
- Supports all levels: Elementary / Ibtedayi → Masters / Kamil Madrasah
- Teachers and administrators can expand the institution list in real time

---

### 👩 FEMALE-SPECIFIC FEATURES (Private)

- Dedicated **Menstruation Tracker** — completely private, never leaves your device
- Toggle individual prayers as skipped during a cycle — **zero streak or XP penalty**
- Automatic cycle length learning to suggest period start/end
- Choose between 30-day or 40-day challenge based on your cycle
- Menstruation data is **never synced, never shared, never sent anywhere**

---

### 🕋 FRIDAY MODE

- On every Friday, **Dhuhr is automatically replaced by Jumu'ah**
- Special golden UI theme activates on Fridays
- Friday-specific XP and completion states

---

### 📖 KNOWLEDGE BANK

- Full step-by-step guides for: **Wudu, Salah, Sujud, Qiyam al-Layl, Adhan**
- Animated SVG illustrations for each step
- Arabic recitation text included where applicable
- Available in both **English and Bengali (বাংলা)**

---

### 🌙 DAILY QUOTES

- A new Hadith or Quranic verse appears every day on the home screen
- Rotates through 7 curated Islamic quotes (one per day of the week)
- Fully bilingual — displays in your chosen language

---

### 📊 STATISTICS & CALENDAR

- Monthly calendar view showing synced, partial, and missed days
- Weekly prayer completion bar chart
- Per-prayer completion rate across your full history
- Best streak, total XP, perfect days, and Fajr on-time count

---

### 📝 NOTEPAD

- Built-in personal note system with title + body
- Expand/collapse long notes with "See more / See less"
- Archive notes instead of deleting them
- Notes stored locally and included in JSON export

---

### 🔔 IN-APP NOTIFICATIONS

- Activity log with a notification bell (top left of home screen)

- System generates notifications for: sync completion, achievements, streak milestones

### 💾 DATA MANAGEMENT

- **Export** your full data as a `.json` file at any time
- **Import** a backup `.json` to restore your data
