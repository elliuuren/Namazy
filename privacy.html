1. Introduction

Namazy ("the App") is a free Islamic prayer tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and what rights you have regarding your data. By using Namazy, you agree to this policy.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Data Stored Locally on Your Device Only

The following data is stored exclusively in your device's local storage (browser localStorage / app storage) and is never transmitted to any external server:

Your first name Personalized greetings

2.2 Data Sent to External Services

a) Prayer Times (Aladhan.com API)

  • What is sent: Your GPS latitude and longitude
  • Why: To calculate accurate local prayer times
  • When: Once per day when you open the app with location permission granted
  • Aladhan Privacy Policy: https://aladhan.com/privacy-policy
  • You can disable this: Deny location permission. Prayer times will use default estimates.

b) Progress Sync (Google Sheets via Apps Script)

  • What is sent: Name, streak count, total XP, level, days logged, perfect days, is-student flag, academy name, class, roll number, challenge completion status, app version, sync date
  • What is NOT sent: Menstruation data, notes, full prayer history, passwords, device identifiers
  • Why: Allows the developer (Elliuuren) to monitor challenge participation for academy leaderboard management
  • When: When you tap "Sync Daily Data"
  • You can disable this: The sync is automatic on each daily sync. If you do not sync, no data is sent.

c) Email (Report an Issue / Claim Authentication)

  • What is sent: Your typed message, selected issue type, app version, streak, XP, device user-agent
  • Why: To process your support request or shield authentication
  • When: Only when you manually tap "Send Report" or "Submit Authentication"
  • This opens your device's email app — you control whether to send it

d) AI / Gemini API (if enabled)

The app does not currently use an AI feature by default. If added in future versions, this policy will be updated.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID, etc.)
  • Browsing history or cross-app behavior
  • Financial or payment information
  • Biometric data
  • Passwords (admin password is hashed using SHA-256 locally)
  • Photos, camera, or microphone data
  • Contact lists
  • Precise location beyond what is needed for prayer times
  • Any data from children under 13 knowingly

5. Data Retention

  • Local data: Stored on your device until you use Hard System Reset or uninstall the app
  • Google Sheets: Progress data sent at each sync is retained by the developer for leaderboard and challenge tracking purposes. You may request deletion by emailing easanians@yahoo.com
  • Aladhan API: Prayer time requests are not logged by the app. Aladhan's own retention policy applies
  • Notification log: Stored locally, capped at 50 entries, auto-cleared on Hard Reset

6. Children's Privacy

Namazy is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided data through this app, please contact us at easanians@yahoo.com and we will take steps to remove it.

Users aged 13–17 should use the app with parental awareness.

7. Data Security

  • All local data is stored in the device's sandboxed localStorage — inaccessible to other apps
  • Admin password is hashed client-side using SHA-256 before comparison — never stored in plaintext
  • Google Sheets sync uses HTTPS
  • Menstruation data has zero external exposure by design

8. Your Rights

  • Access your data: All your data is visible within the app itself
  • Delete your data: Use Settings → Hard System Reset to wipe all local data
  • Request deletion of synced data: Email easanians@yahoo.com
  • Withdraw consent to location: Deny/revoke location permission in your device settings
  • Withdraw consent to notifications: Revoke notification permission in device settings
